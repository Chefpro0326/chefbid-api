Privacy Policy

Effective: November 18, 2026

What We Collect

ChefBid PRO collects information you provide when creating an account — your name, email address, and password. We also store event data, vendor entries, and cost analyses you create within the app. This data is stored in our secure online database so you can access it from any device.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide and improve the ChefBid PRO service. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not display advertising.

Data Storage

Your account and saved events are stored in a secure online database provided by Supabase. Passwords are handled by Supabase's authentication service and are never stored in plain text. Each account can only access its own data. Some app preferences may also be kept in your browser's local storage.

Third-Party Services

ChefBid PRO uses the Anthropic Claude API to power AI features. When you use AI analysis, your menu descriptions and event details are sent to Anthropic for processing. Please review Anthropic's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle data.

Your account and event data are stored with Supabase. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card details. Our site is hosted on Render and Railway.

Cookies

ChefBid PRO does not use tracking cookies. We use browser storage to keep you signed in and save your preferences.

Your Rights

You may delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at info@chefbidpro.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at info@chefbidpro.com.
